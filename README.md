## 👨🏽‍💻 About Me

I'm a Full-Stack Software Developer, Tech Educator and Mentor based in Mombasa, Kenya.

I enjoy building scalable web applications, solving real-world problems through technology, and helping aspiring developers transition from learning programming to building production-ready software.

My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering, technology education, entrepreneurship and innovation**.

- 🚀 Founder & CEO at **Mwenaro Technologies**.
- 🎓 Founder & Lead Instructor at **Mwenaro Academy**.
- 👨🏽‍🏫 Instructor at **GOMYCODE**, facilitating practical software development learning sessions.
- 💻 Lead Developer at **Mombasa Digital Solutions**.
- 🌍 Passionate about building technology solutions and expanding access to software engineering education across Africa.
- 🤝 Interested in open-source collaboration, developer communities and mentorship.
- 🔬 Exploring AI-powered applications, RAG systems and workflow automation.
